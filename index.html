The problem

OIL logs a large number of Unsafe-Act/Unsafe-Condition (UA/UC), near-miss and incident reports. Most analysis is manual and done in periodic batches.

Buried precursors

A routine-looking free-text report can hide a SIF precursor that goes unnoticed for a long time.

Severity ≠ root cause

A low-severity incident does not necessarily share root causes with a fatality, yet it may still carry fatal potential.

Patterns stay hidden

Recurring combinations of activity, location and barrier failure are easy to miss when reports are reviewed one by one.

Our solution: Lanezy

An AI/NLP safety intelligence system that reads reports, flags possible SIF precursors, maps Life-Saving Rules and surfaces high-risk patterns.

How it works

Seven-step safety report intelligence workflow, ending in human review.

  1. Safety reportRaw UA/UC & near-miss text
  2. NLP preprocessingClean & normalise text
  3. SIF classificationSIF-potential or not
  4. Rule mappingLife-Saving Rule tags
  5. Pattern detectionActivity/location/barrier trends
  6. Risk prioritisationPrecursor-density ranking
  7. HSE dashboardReview & intervene

    System architecture

    Layers from the proposed technical approach.

    1. Input & dataOIL UA/UC, near-miss & incident reports
    2. NLP processingPython, preprocessing, embeddings/features
    3. SIF classifierSupervised NLP/ML model
    4. Rule mappingMulti-label / semantic matching
    5. Pattern analysisClustering, frequency & association
    6. DashboardWeb dashboard, charts & tables
    7. HSE reviewHuman-in-the-loop decision

    Impact & benefits

    Safety

    Surface SIF precursors before they escalate into high-consequence events.

    Faster HSE review

    Less manual scanning of large volumes of free-text reports.

    Better prioritisation

    Rank sites and activities by precursor density and recurring patterns.

    Standardised analysis

    Automatic mapping to IOGP Life-Saving Rules.

    Pattern visibility

    Connect repeated activity, location and barrier-failure signals.

    Continuous improvement

    Reviewed outcomes feed back into labels, rules and model performance.

    Technology, risks & safeguards

    Planned stack

    Python · NLP/ML · supervised classifier · multi-label / semantic matching · clustering · web dashboard. This prototype: HTML, CSS, vanilla JS.

    Safety-first design

    Tuned for safety-sensitive recall, uncertain cases reviewed, no fully automated final decisions.

    Explainable & controlled

    Shows important terms, matched rules, confidence and similar reports. Controlled/on-premise deployment preferred.
